## 4. Eligibility and application

- Credit is for duly registered members; other registered cooperatives may be granted facilities
  under the Act and the 2026 By-law (1.2.1).
- Criteria: eligibility, type and size of loan, purpose and utilisation, source of repayment,
  ability to repay (Maximum Deductible Limit), savings balance, global limit, guarantors,
  employment and joining dates, date of birth (1.3.1 a to j).
- A request that fails the criteria is rejected and the applicant notified (1.3.2).
- Special Withdrawal requires six months of continuous saving (2.1.1).
- Special Loan requires one year of contributions (2.3.1).
- Quick Credit requires evidence of ability to repay within the period (2.4.1).
- Project Loan requires 30% unencumbered savings of project value (2.6.2).
- End of Service Loan requires a written application to the President, referral to the Credit
  Committee, and CBN HR confirmation of deduction from final entitlement (2.7.2).
- Guarantors: members under five years with the Cooperative provide one for every facility except
  Normal Loans; the guarantor must be at least one grade above the borrower and may guarantee only
  one member at a time (4.6.1, 4.6.2, 4.7.1, 4.7.2).
- Security: any facility above net savings must be secured by a qualifying pledge (4.8.1), listed
  in 4.9.1 (a) to (d).
- Terms are disclosed on the e-portal or application form before the applicant agrees (4.9.2, 4.9.3).

## 5. Repayment and recovery

- Periods run from the effective date of disbursement: Normal 12 months, Special 36 months,
  Investment 60 months (4.1.3, conflicting), Quick Credit 3 months (4.1.1 to 4.1.4).
- Sources: salary deductions, annual allowances, monthly pension, other approved sources (4.2.1).
- Set-off against savings for long outstanding loans (4.2.1).
- Recall on resignation or retirement, dismissal or termination (4.3.1).
- Default: the balance is rolled over at a new rate set by the MC from the day after the due date
  (4.5.1). No fixed penal percentage is stated.
- Recovery order: annual allowance bulk payment, then final entitlements, then other pledges
  (6.2.2). Maturity notice at 30 days before due date, payment request letter at 33 days, ageing
  report at 90 days, Credit Committee advice at 93 days and above, legal action notice signed by
  the President (6.2.4 to 6.2.11).
- Retirees: total loan including interest not more than 90% of combined savings and bond
  investment; recovery from those balances (8.1.1 to 8.1.3).
- Pensioners: pledged monthly pension deductions sufficient for all obligations; interest on a
  reducing balance (9.1.1 to 9.1.3).
- Portfolio: outstanding days not more than 30; NPL ratio not more than 1% (5.0.1, 5.0.2).

Compliance Officer, four other members appointed by the MC (one pensioner, one retiree, two
serving staff including one junior), Assistant Secretary 2 as Secretary, Head of Operations as a
non-voting member (3.3.1).

## 7. Insider lending

Loans to MC members, executive management and staff are on the same terms, conditions, credit
evaluation and recovery procedures as other members. The Internal Auditor reports insider
outstanding balances to the Audit Committee every quarter (7.1.1 to 7.1.3).
